**120. SpecimenMatch — Cross-Institution Lab Specimen Identity Resolution Under Ambiguous Demographics**
**Problem Statement**
A shared external laboratory processing samples from multiple hospitals must match each returning result to the correct patient record, but different hospitals' systems may hold slightly different demographic details for the same patient, or, more dangerously, two different patients across institutions may share very similar demographic details, and an incorrect match here means a lab result is attributed to the wrong person entirely, a serious specimen-labeling problem that has nothing to do with interpreting the result itself. Design a system that resolves simulated lab result identity matching across multiple hospitals with realistic demographic inconsistencies and near-identical patient pairs, correctly matching results to the right patient and explicitly flagging cases where a confident match cannot be safely established rather than guessing. A convincing demonstration should present both a correct match despite inconsistent demographics and a genuinely ambiguous near-identical pair, with the system correctly distinguishing the two.
